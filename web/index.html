Exchange Rates & Tax Calculation

Fetches official USD/NIS rates from the Bank of Israel for each trade date, then runs both Israeli and US calculations.

🏦 Bank of Israel Exchange Rate Service
Methodology: Each trade's USD gain is converted to NIS using the Bank of Israel official rate on the actual sale date (Israeli Tax Ordinance §207A). For weekends and public holidays, the last preceding banking-day rate is used — the standard approach accepted by both the ITA and the IRS. Israeli salary is converted using the annual average rate (IRS Rev. Rul. 2008-38).
